The 58 tables that hold data

Every table the seeded school actually uses, what each one is for, the columns that carry meaning, and an entity diagram per area. The full reference covers all 86 tables and all 990 columns; this is the part you keep open.

Four columns are left out below because they repeat on almost every table: id, school_id, created_at and updated_at. Stating them once rather than 58 times removes 225 of 611 rows. school_id is the tenant key, so its edge is left off the diagrams too, which would otherwise draw it on nearly every table.

Four tables are exceptions, deliberately. permissions, scheduled_jobs, schools carry no school_id: they derive from TimestampedBase rather than TenantBase, because schools is the tenant, and the permission vocabulary and the job schedule belong to the software rather than to any one school. alembic_version has none of the four — it is Alembic's own bookkeeping and not part of the product.
