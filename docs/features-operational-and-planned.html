How to read this document: This guide explains what the Sunrise Public School management system does in clear, everyday terms. For every section, it details "What You Can View" (the exact information, tables, and summaries shown on the screen) and "What You Can Do" (the tasks, approvals, exports, and actions you can perform).
Part 1 Operational Features: Available on the Website Today
1. Executive Dashboard & Leadership Command Center
/dashboard
👁 What You Can View
  • School Vital Signs: Live counts of Total Students (100), Active Teachers (12), Classrooms (10), and Total Realized Fees (₹4,72,890.00).
  • Fees Overview Card: Real-time split showing Fees Collected (49%) vs Fees Remaining (₹5,00,040.00) with a visual progress bar.
  • Grievance Activity: Live counter chips showing Open, In Progress, and Resolved complaints from teachers and parents.
  • Quick Notice & Holiday Board: Summary of upcoming official school holidays and recent parent circulars.
⚡ What You Can Do
  • Click "Fees Remaining": Instantly opens a complete roster of all students with pending fee dues.
  • Export Defaulters Report: Download a full 10-column spreadsheet report with one click, showing pending amounts, due dates, and grand total.
  • Open Grievances: Click any complaint to read the full conversation thread between parents, teachers, and leadership.
  • Reply & Assign: Write a response back to the parent/teacher, assign the ticket to another staff member, and mark it resolved.
2. New Student Admissions & Seat Intake Management
/admission
👁 What You Can View
  • Class Seat Capacity: Exact approved seats, enrolled children, and remaining vacancies for each grade (Nursery through Class 10).
  • Admission Funnel: Visual drop-off funnel showing how many leads moved from Inquiry → Application → Test → Offer → Final Enrolment.
  • Active Academic Cycle: Which session is currently accepting applications (e.g. 2025-26 or 2026-27).
⚡ What You Can Do
  • Edit Class Capacities: Adjust the maximum seat ceiling for any class as physical room availability permits.
  • Monitor Over-Enrolment: The system automatically blocks admitting more children than the physical seat cap allows.
  • Track Cycle Progress: Inspect conversion percentages to know which classes still have vacant seats.
3. Walk-In & Phone Inquiry Register
/admission/enquiries
👁 What You Can View
  • Inquiry Log: Chronological log of visiting parents, telephone queries, prospective student names, and desired class.
  • Follow-Up Notes: Detailed conversation history recorded by reception staff (e.g. "Parent called asking for fee structure").
  • Status Badges: Whether the inquiry is New, Followed-up, Converted, or Disqualified.
⚡ What You Can Do
  • Log Walk-In Visits: Receptionists can record parent phone numbers and questions within 30 seconds.
  • Record Follow-Ups: Schedule call-back reminders and log staff notes directly onto the parent's file.
  • One-Click Conversion: Click "Convert to Application" to prefill the formal admission form without re-typing details.
4. Complete 360° Applicant Dossier
/admission/applications
👁 What You Can View
  • Applicant Profile (7 Tabs): Personal biodata, residential address, parent occupation, and previous school transfer details.
  • Document Checklist: Verified status of Birth Certificate, Aadhaar (last 4 digits), Transfer Certificate, and Report Cards.
  • Medical & Allergy Notes: Blood group, known allergies, medical emergency notes, and family physician contacts.
  • Sibling Links: Identifies if an elder brother or sister is already studying in the school for fee concession eligibility.
⚡ What You Can Do
  • Verify Documents: Mark certificates as Verified or Incomplete with official auditor remarks.
  • Record Admission Test Scores: Enter written test and interview scorecards directly on the candidate's sheet.
  • Issue Admission Offers: Approve applications and generate official offer letters.
  • Convert to Enrolled Student: Once the admission fee is cleared, finalize enrollment to assign their permanent 10-digit Admission ID.
5. Merit Ranking, Selection & Waiting List
/admission/merit & /admission/waitlist
👁 What You Can View
  • Merit Lists: Candidates ordered automatically by their total test and interview scores per class.
  • Ordered Waiting List: Candidates who qualified but did not receive an immediate seat, ranked strictly by merit.
  • Capacity Meters: Live visual bars indicating whether seats are full or open for promotion.
⚡ What You Can Do
  • Batch Approvals: Select the top 20 candidates and issue admission offers in one batch with a shared audit reason.
  • Promote from Waitlist: When an admitted candidate drops out, click "Promote" to immediately offer the vacant seat to the next student in line.
6. Student Directory & Profile Records
/students
👁 What You Can View
  • School Roster: Complete roster of all 100 students across classes 1-A to 10-A.
  • Student Details: Permanent Admission Number, class section, guardian name, phone number, and fee clearance status.
  • Class Filter: Instantly filter the roster by any individual class (e.g., show only Class 10-A).
⚡ What You Can Do
  • Real-Time Search: Search for any student in under 2 seconds by typing their name, admission number, or father's phone.
  • Profile Drilldown: Click any student to review their complete academic records, past attendance, and family details.
7. Teachers & Staff Directory
/teachers
👁 What You Can View
  • Staff Directory: Complete listing of 16 school employees (teachers, administrators, bus drivers, and support attendants).
  • Assignments: Which teacher is the official Class Teacher, which subjects they teach, and their department.
  • Contact Details: Official employee codes, phone numbers, and email addresses.
⚡ What You Can Do
  • Find Contact Info: Quickly look up an educator's phone number during emergency office situations.
  • Verify Subject Load: Check which teacher is responsible for specific subjects like Science or Mathematics.
8. Classrooms, Subject Teachers & Timetable Grid
/classes
👁 What You Can View
  • Class Sections: Class 1-A to 10-A with designated Class Teachers and total enrolled students.
  • Subject Allocations: List of all curriculum subjects (Math, English, Science, Social Science, Hindi) and their assigned instructors.
  • Weekly Timetable Grid: Period-by-period schedule from Monday to Saturday for every section.
⚡ What You Can Do
  • Assign Class Teachers: Change or appoint the lead teacher for any classroom section.
  • Review Schedule Clashes: Inspect the timetable grid to verify that teachers are not double-booked across different rooms.
9. Daily Student Attendance Register
/attendance
👁 What You Can View
  • Daily Roll-Call Sheet: Roster of children with status badges: Present, Absent, Late, or Approved Leave.
  • Short Attendance Warning: Immediate warning alert highlighting any student whose attendance drops below CBSE's mandatory 75%.
  • School Closed Safeguard: Clearly flags official school holidays and declared weather closures.
⚡ What You Can Do
  • Mark Roll-Call: Class teachers or clerks can record attendance with a single click per child.
  • Generate Absence Lists: Pull a list of all children absent on today's date so office staff can phone their parents.
10. Examinations, Marks Entry & CBSE Report Cards
/exams
👁 What You Can View
  • Exam Datesheets: Schedule of upcoming Half-Yearly, Unit Tests, and Final Board examinations with dates and max marks.
  • CBSE Grading Rules: Official 8-point grading scale (A1 for 91-100%, A2 for 81-90%, down to E).
  • Marks Roster: Grid listing all students in a section with their individual marks, Absent flags, or Medical Exemptions.
  • Official CBSE Report Card Preview: Term 1 & Term 2 marks, internal assessments (PT, Notebook, Subject Enrichment), and grades.
⚡ What You Can Do
  • Enter Marks in Grid: Input scores directly into a spreadsheet-like interface with instant score validation.
  • Lock Exam Papers: Permanently lock marks after verification. Once locked, changing a mark requires an audited reason.
  • Check Publication Readiness: The system automatically verifies that all papers are scored before issuing report cards.
  • Withhold Report Cards for Dues: Automatically marks report cards as "Withheld" if a student has unpaid school fees.
11. Student Fees, Ledgers & Counter Collections
/fees & /fees/ledger
👁 What You Can View
  • Financial Snapshot: Total fees billed vs realized cash collections for the academic session.
  • Student Account Ledger: Complete chronological financial history for any child showing every bill and receipt.
  • Late Fee Calculations: Automatic late-fee charges (₹300 after 5 days, then ₹100/day capped at 50% of the invoice).
  • Fee Receipts: Official numbered receipts with transaction date, payment mode (Cash, Cheque, UPI), and clerk ID.
⚡ What You Can Do
  • Generate Monthly Invoices: Raise tuition and transport fee bills for all classes in a single automated batch.
  • Collect Payments: Record counter fee payments against specific invoice heads with instant receipt issuance.
  • Audited Payment Reversals: If a cheque bounces or an error is made, reverse the entry with a mandatory typed audit explanation.
  • Close Accounting Periods: Lock completed calendar months to prevent unauthorized backdated edits by clerks.
12. Fee Defaulter Chasing Register
/fees/defaulters
👁 What You Can View
  • Worst-First Ranking: Complete list of all families behind on payments, ranked from highest overdue balance down.
  • Guardian Emergency Contacts: Displays father's and mother's mobile numbers directly alongside the amount owed.
  • Months Overdue: Exactly how many billing cycles (1 month, 2 months, 3+ months) the student has missed.
⚡ What You Can Do
  • Filter by Amount: Set a threshold (e.g. show only students owing more than ₹5,000) for prioritized chasing.
  • Phone Calling Roster: Administrative staff can sit with this screen and ring parents directly with the exact figure at hand.
13. Classroom Supplies & Lab Stock Management
/inventory
👁 What You Can View
  • Supplies Catalogue: Inventory of laboratory chemicals, glassware, science models, math kits, chalks, and dusters.
  • Low-Stock Highlights: Real-time alerts when supplies fall below minimum threshold (e.g. Printer Paper < 2 reams).
  • Pending Approval Counter: Number of purchase and item-issue requests awaiting leadership approval.
  • Department Locations: Which room holds which material (e.g., Chemistry Lab, Admin Store, Medical Room).
⚡ What You Can Do
  • Add New Stock: Register new supplies, set units (bottles, boxes, kits), and define minimum reorder thresholds.
  • Approve / Reject Requests: Review teacher requests for art supplies or lab acids and approve distributions.
  • Receive Mobile Flags: When teachers report low chalk or broken beakers from their phone, it appears here instantly.
14. Notice Board & Parent Circulars
/notices
👁 What You Can View
  • Published Circulars: Chronological board of school notices (exam schedules, winter vacations, staff meetings).
  • Target Audience Tags: Whether the notice was published for Everyone, Parents Only, Teachers Only, or Specific Classes.
⚡ What You Can Do
  • Compose Notices: Write announcements with rich titles and body text, and select the exact recipient group.
  • Audited Archival: Delete or archive outdated circulars with a permanent recorded reason.
15. School Bus Routes, Stops & Capacity Tracking
/transport
👁 What You Can View
  • Active Routes: Bus route names (e.g. Gomti Nagar Express, Aliganj Route), driver name, and vehicle registration number.
  • Stops & Timings: Sequential pick-up stops along the route with scheduled morning and afternoon timings.
  • Seating Occupancy: Total bus seats vs enrolled student passengers (e.g. 28 / 32 seats filled).
  • Interactive Map: Visual Leaflet map showing Lucknow bus stops and route paths.
⚡ What You Can Do
  • Assign Students to Stops: Allocate newly admitted students to their nearest designated bus stop.
  • Prevent Overcrowding: The system warns administrators if assigning another child exceeds the vehicle's safe seat limit.
16. Official Reports Library & Audit Exports
/reports
👁 What You Can View
  • 21 Standard School Reports: Categorized across Fees, Attendance, Academics, Timetable, Transport, Communication, and HR.
  • Interactive Data Previews: Live tabular formatting with student names, dates, amounts, and contact details.
  • KPI Stat Cards: High-level visual metrics for quick executive reviews.
⚡ What You Can Do
  • Dynamic Filtering: Run reports by specific date ranges, classes, or fee balance thresholds.
  • Export to CSV / Excel: One-click button to download clean, audit-ready spreadsheets for board meetings or tax filings.
17. Mobile App Access: Teacher & Parent Portals
mobile/(teacher) & mobile/(parent)
👁 What You Can View
  • Teacher Leave Portal: Personal leave history, pending applications, approval/rejection status, and admin remarks.
  • Substitute Duty Roster: "Duties" tab showing assigned proxy classroom periods covering for absent colleagues.
  • Teacher Supply Catalogue: Teachers can inspect lab and classroom materials right from their phone.
  • Grievance Portals: Teachers and parents can log issues, track resolution states, and exchange messages with school leadership.
⚡ What You Can Do
  • Teacher Self-Service Leave: Apply for full-day or consecutive multi-day leave with reason (strictly non-cancellable once submitted).
  • In-App Notifications: Instant alert banners when leave requests are approved or rejected, or when substitution duties are assigned.
  • Flag Diminishing Stock: Tap to alert the school office that classroom supplies, chalk, or reagents are depleted.
18. Staff Leave Management & Timetable Substitution Gate
/staff-leave & mobile/(teacher)/leave
👁 What You Can View
  • Staff Leave Queue: Chronological register of teacher leave applications showing applicant name, leave dates, reason, and status (Applied, Approved, Rejected).
  • Dynamic Substitution Matrix: Affected periods breakdown (Period #, Class, Subject, Scheduled Teacher) derived from master timetable.
  • Coverage Status & Safety Gate: Visual progress gauge (0%, 50%, 100%) and color-coded alert banner. Approval button remains strictly locked until 100% covered.
  • Free Teacher Availability: System dynamically calculates free, non-conflicting faculty members for each affected period.
⚡ What You Can Do
  • Assign Provisional Substitutes: Select available teachers from dropdowns for each uncovered classroom period.
  • Approve Leave with 100% Coverage: Once all slots are covered, click "Approve Leave & Confirm Substitutions" to commit date-bound substitutions.
  • Reject Application & Purge: Reject an application with an audited justification (e.g. critical exam duty), immediately purging provisional substitutions and preserving master timetable slots.
  • Mobile Duty Reflection: Assigned substitute teachers automatically receive in-app duty notices on their mobile device.
19. Teacher Recruitment & Candidate Intake Dossier
/recruitment
👁 What You Can View
  • Recruitment Pipeline: Visual stage cards tracking Applied, Shortlisted, Interviewed, Offered, Hired, and Rejected applicants.
  • CBSE A4 Application Preview: Printable standard candidate dossier with passport photograph box, academic/teaching credentials, prior school experience, and signature blocks.
  • Offer & Salary Terms: Offered designation, department, monthly CTC salary (₹), and scheduled joining date.
⚡ What You Can Do
  • Front-Desk Candidate Intake: Receptionist can register walk-in teacher applicants and print official CBSE A4 submission dossiers within 1 minute.
  • RBAC Pipeline Isolation: Receptionist is strictly isolated from hiring actions; only School Admin can shortlist, offer, or hire.
  • Issue Job Offer: Admin sets designation and salary terms via "Issue Job Offer" modal.
  • One-Click Onboarding: Confirming joining automatically provisions active teacher employee records and login credentials (TCH0xx / Teacher@123) for immediate mobile/web login.
20. Payroll Processing & Teacher Salary Disbursal
/payroll
👁 What You Can View
  • Monthly Payroll Batches: Total school wage bill, employee headcount, gross earnings, statutory deductions, and net payout.
  • Salary Structure Registry: Compensation packages by designation (PGT ₹42,000, TGT ₹32,000, PRT ₹19,500, Driver ₹16,000).
  • Statutory Breakdown: Itemized Provident Fund (EPF 12%), Employee State Insurance (ESI 0.75%), TDS, and Professional Tax.
  • Printable CBSE Payslip: Official Lucknow school letterhead, earnings vs deductions grid, amount in words, and authorized sign-off.
⚡ What You Can Do
  • Run Monthly Payroll: One-click batch calculation applying attendance loss-of-pay and generating itemized payslips.
  • Export Bank Disbursal CSV: Download electronic bank transfer spreadsheet in standard NEFT/RTGS format for batch bank processing.
  • Print Official Payslips: Print individual or batch staff payslips via browser print with official school seals.
21. Session Rollover & Promotion Wizard
/admin/session-rollover
👁 What You Can View
  • 4-Step Wizard Workflow: Session selector (2025-26 to 2026-27), Class Card Selector, Roster Review & Overrides, and Confirmation.
  • Cohort Progression Grid: Class roster displaying student name, admission ID, current class/sec, proposed next class/sec, and new roll number.
  • Decision Outcome Badges: Visual chips for Promote, Detain, Pass Out (Class 10), and Transfer Out.
  • Capacity & Blocker Alerts: Live indicators warning of section capacity overflows or unallocated students.
⚡ What You Can Do
  • Batch Student Progression: Automatically advance entire classes (Class 1-A to Class 2-A) while reallocating roll numbers alphabetically.
  • Individual Overrides: Selectively detain failed students or mark departing students as Pass Out / Transfer Out.
  • Audited Safety Gate: Enforces typed PROMOTE confirmation with a user-entered audit justification saved to audit_log.
  • Preserve Historical Ledger: Seamlessly creates new academic year enrolments while keeping past grades, attendance, and fee history intact.
